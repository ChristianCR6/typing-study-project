Typing Study

Welcome

Thank you for taking part in this study on typing performance. The session will take approximately 12–15 minutes from start to finish:

  • A short information page (this screen and the next)
  • A few quick questions about your background
  • A 1-minute practice run to get used to the interface
  • Two 5-minute typing tasks, with a short break between them

Please complete the session in a quiet environment without distractions, using a physical keyboard. When you are ready, click below.

Information & consent

What this study is about

This research compares two types of typing task: copy-based typing (reproducing a passage of text shown on screen) and prompt-based typing (writing a free response to a written prompt). The aim is to understand whether typing performance differs between these two conditions.

What you will do

You will complete one short practice round followed by two five-minute typing tasks. Each task will be one of the two types described above. The order is assigned by the system.

What data is recorded

For each task, the application records the keys you press, the timing of each keystroke, and the final text you produce. The optional background information you provide on the next page is also stored. Your participant ID is the only identifier — your name is not collected.

Your rights

Participation is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page. The data file is exported at the end of the session and is provided to you to forward to the researcher; if you change your mind, simply do not send it. The collected data will be used only for this academic project and any associated assessment.

Participant information

Please enter the participant ID provided by the researcher. The questions below are optional and can be left as “prefer not to say”.

Practice round (1 minute)

Before the recorded tasks you will do a brief one-minute practice round to get used to the interface. This is just a chance to familiarise yourself with the typing area, the timer, and the layout of the page. Nothing you type during practice is recorded or analysed.

Once you press your first key the timer will begin counting down from one minute. You can type whatever you like — random words, sentences, or anything at all — until the timer runs out. Pasting and dragging text into the box are disabled.
